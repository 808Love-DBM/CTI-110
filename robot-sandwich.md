# My PB & J Robot

### Assumptions
- an unobstructed counter exists with the following items laid out
 - Jar of PB
 - Jar of Jelly
 - Two slices of bread set flat side-by-side on a plate
 - Clean Butter knife
 - 2 Napkin
 - Trash can at end of counter on floor
 - Robot is charged and ready to make a PB & J sandwhich
 


### Numbered Steps
 1. Pick up PB container off the counter with right hand and remove (screw off) lid with left hand
 2. Place opened PB container and lid back on the counter
 3. Pick up Jelly jar off the counter with right hand and remove (screw off) lid with left hand
 4. Place opened Jelly jar and lid back on the counter
 5. Pick up the butter knife off countr with right hand 
 6. Place left hand around PB container and hold container
 7. Insirt butter knife into PB and scoop out 1 tablespoon of PB 
 8. Let go of PB container with left hand
 9. Take left hand and gently hold bread thats on the left side of plate 
 10. Take scooped PB in right hand and spread the piece of bread that is on the left side of plate
 11. (Loop) while spreading PB gently on the bread until surface is completely covered
 12. Stop spreading PB 
 13. Let go of bread with left hand and pick up napkin off counter with left hand
 14. Take 1 napkin and wipe off PB from butter knife in right hand
 15. Throw napkin that has wiped PB on it away in trash can at end of counter on floor
 16. Place left hand around Jelly jar and hold container
 17. Insert butter knife into Jelly jar and scoop out 1 tablespoon of Jelly
 17. Let go of Jelly jar with left hand
 18. Take left hand and gently hold bread thats on the right side of plate
 19. Take scooped Jelly in right hand spread the piece of bread that is on the right side of plate
 20. (Loop) while spreading Jelly gently on the bread until surface is completely covered
 21. Stop spreading Jelly
 22. Let go of bread with left hand and pick 2nd napkin off counter with left hand
 23. Take 2nd napkin and wipe off Jelly from butter knife that is in right hand
 24. Throw napkin that has wiped Jelly on it away in trash can at end of counter on floor
 24. Place butter knife on counter from right hand
 25. Take right hand and pick up the right piece of bread that has Jelly facing up
 26. Take left hand and pick up the left side piece of bread that has PB facing up
 27. Place bread together with PB & Jelly sides facing each other with all corners touching
 28. Have completed PB and Jelly in right hand
 29. Take right hand and place PB and Jelly on center of plate
 30. Clean up area 
 31. Pick up and place Jelly jar in refridgerater
 32. Pick up and place PB in cabinet
 33. Place butter knife in sink
 



 ## Exit Conditions
 - Plate with PB & Jelly sandwich are the only items on counter
